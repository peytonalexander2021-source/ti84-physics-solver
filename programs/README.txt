PHYSICS SOLVER for TI-84 Plus CE  (Physics I: motion, vectors, forces)
=======================================================================

What it does
------------
Pick what you are SOLVING FOR, then what the problem GIVES you. It asks for
the numbers, shows the formula, plugs in the numbers with units, and gives
the answer with correct sig figs. No letter typing needed.

Covers:
- Speed/velocity, average velocity, x-t and v-t graphs, a(t)/v(t)/x(t) formulas
- Constant acceleration (any 3 of 5), free fall, thrown straight up
- Vectors: components, magnitude/angle (with quadrant steps), adding vectors, 3D,
  dot and cross product, right triangles
- Projectiles: horizontal launch, plane drop, angled launch
- Relative motion: boat/swimmer in a river, plane in wind
- Forces: F = ma, push/pull at an angle, friction, sliding to a stop, ramps,
  elevators/scales, pulleys and Atwood machines, 2 blocks in contact, drag and
  terminal speed, net force from many forces
- Circular motion: centripetal force, period/RPM, max speed on curves and hills
- Unit converter, metric prefixes, sig figs, and a concept cheat sheet

Files (send ALL 7 to the calculator)
------------------------------------
PHYSICS.8xp  <- the one you run
PHYSB.8xp PHYSC.8xp PHYSD.8xp PHYSE.8xp PHYSF.8xp SIGFIGS.8xp  <- helpers

How to install (5 minutes)
--------------------------
1. Install TI Connect CE (free) from education.ti.com.
2. Plug the calculator into the computer with its USB cable and turn it on.
3. In TI Connect CE, open "Calculator Explorer", drag all 7 .8xp files in,
   and click Send. (Sending the helpers to Archive is fine and saves RAM.)
4. On the calculator: press [prgm], pick PHYSICS, press [enter] [enter].

Works on TI-84 Plus CE / CE-T. Plain TI-BASIC: no jailbreak needed.
Uses degrees and g = 9.8 m/s^2.

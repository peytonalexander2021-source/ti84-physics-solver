Formula Foundry · Free app · TI-84 Plus CE · Physics I

Type what the problem gives you. PHYSPRO solves for everything else.

PHYSPRO is a physics formula solver for the TI-84 Plus CE. Fill in the values the problem gives you and leave the rest blank. It chains the formulas, finds every missing value, and shows each step: the formula, the plug-in line with units, and the answer rounded to the right sig figs.

PHYSPRO (the app shown above)

Download PHYSPRO (5 files)
  1. Install TI Connect CE (free).
  2. Send all 5 files to the calculator's Archive.
  3. On OS 5.5 or newer, C apps need arTIfiCE to launch. Follow its guide, then start PHYSPRO.
No jailbreak

PHYSICS Classic (plain TI-BASIC)

Download Classic (7 files)
  1. Menus organized by what you're solving for: motion, vectors, projectiles, forces, circular motion.
  2. Send all 7 files, then press prgm, pick PHYSICS, and press enter twice.
